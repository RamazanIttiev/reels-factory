Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. What we collect

scalelab reels does not collect personal data from visitors or viewers. The tool is operated only by its owner.

2. TikTok data

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the account name). This is used only to upload the owner's videos to that account.

3. Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own systems. They are not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. The only external services that receive data are TikTok and Instagram, for the purpose of publishing the owner's videos.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
